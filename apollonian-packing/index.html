Standard Explorer

An interactive explorer for Apollonian gaskets — fractal circle packings built by repeatedly filling the gaps between mutually tangent circles.

Starting from four mutually tangent circles, Descartes' Circle Theorem gives a simple formula for a new circle tangent to any three of them. Repeating this in every gap, forever, produces the gasket: an infinite, self-similar packing whose circles shrink without bound.

Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan — new circles are generated as you go, so you can zoom in indefinitely. Use the panel to type in your own four tangent curvatures, pick a preset, control how many circles are generated up front, switch between filled and outline rendering, or turn on integral mode to see the classic whole-number curvature labels. A curvature of 0 is a straight line, so quadruples like a half-plane or a strip (two parallel lines) work too; curvature fields also accept algebraic expressions such as (1+sqrt(2))^2.

Quadruple

Expressions allowed: + - * / ^ sqrt(). Use 0 for a straight line.
